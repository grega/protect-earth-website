A vitally important part of what Protect Earth does once we’ve put the trees in the ground is maintenance and restocking, there is a lot to be done during this most vulnerable time in the tree’s life.

On this occasion, we placed down mulch mats to suppress the grass growing around the tree. Along with pulling out grass and weeds growing inside the tree guards, whilst making sure the stakes were straight and firm into the ground.

It was identified based on the survival rate of the initial planting that we needed to restock 375 trees and 25 shrubs, with a small team of corporate volunteers over two days we got the job done!
