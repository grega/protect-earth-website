The Cotswold Warden volunteers planted 2000 trees here on another of our farms in the Box and Colerne area, this time near Chippenham, to form a 500m hedgerow.  Hedgerows have been disappearing in the English countryside since the 1940s, and are only recently starting to make a comeback. This hedgerow will act as a barrier and shelter for livestock, as well as providing food, shelter and safe passage for many species of wildlife from mammals to invertebrates, and of course it will help to sequester carbon. Twelve larger trees were also planted to begin a small coppice which will expand over time.
