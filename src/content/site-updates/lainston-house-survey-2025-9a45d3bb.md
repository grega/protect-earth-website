During a surveying trip to our partner sites in the south-east, our site inspector visited the tree planting in the grounds of Lainston House Hotel. Something immediately obvious during the visit was how much the grass and nettles were dominating the saplings, and from a distance no saplings could be seen poking out of the guards.  On closer inspection  we did find a few taller saplings, and quite a few small, but healthy plants still within the guards. In all we discovered a survival rate of 65%, which was disappointing.

This site is well shielded from wind, there was no sign of cattle, and all the stakes and guards were in perfect condition, which is reassuring. The thick grass presents a challenge which we will take into consideration when planning restocking efforts here.
