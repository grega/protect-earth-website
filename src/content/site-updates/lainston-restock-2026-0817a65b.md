We returned to Lainston in February 2026 to restock some of the trees as our survey showed a disappointing loss following a hot summer. Today, we planted 300 new trees on the site. This time we were joined by a corporate group from WorkDry who are clients of Ecologi, one of our funding partners, and there were 3 gardeners onsite that also helped out. The hotel kindly provided us with another excellent lunch, and were more than happy to cater for vegans.

Part of the tasks for the day was to carry out maintenance on the existing trees, from the planting in 2023. We used additional stakes and guards to protect the new trees, and weeded overgrowth and long grass from the original saplings. Doing regular maintenance like this gives the trees a good chance of survival, and keeps the site balanced while they grow.

The feedback from the landowners was good, and all the volunteers had a great day. The general manager of the hotel  sent us a lovely email after the event. “I would like to personally thank you for the time and energy put into organising the tree planting at Lainston a couple of weeks ago. We are very grateful for your support as this part of our estate continues to grow and develop into a beautiful woodland. I am very sorry that I was unable to say hello personally on the day, but I am well aware of the effort that was put in and really would like to say thank you to you and the volunteers for the hard work. Great team effort! I look forward to working to together again the future.:
