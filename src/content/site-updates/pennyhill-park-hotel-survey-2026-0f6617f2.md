This site faces a number of challenges, including deer, flooding and dense gorse. The trees planted amongst the gorse have fared moderately well in some places, thanks to our maintenance visits, but others did less well.  This area in particular will need regular ongoing management and a restock in due course.

The more open areas have done better, especially where we restocked earlier in the year, with a number of healthy trees poking out of the top of their guards. However, the ground here is exceptionally dry and it was sad to see dead trees that had obviously been growing well before this exceptionally hot summer. We also saw several deer on our visit. This area will need a few new saplings to fill in the gaps.

Better news was the numerous signs of natural regeneration. These saplings tend to be hardier and more likely to survive drought conditions. We will be checking on them on our next visit.
