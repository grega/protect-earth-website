Our surveyor was delighted to see how well the trees were doing here, especially the hedgerow which had a survival rate of 96% and the shelterbelt with a survival rate of 97%. Even the woodland area was coping well in the dry conditions with a survival rate of 87%, giving us an overall success rate of 93%. Looking down the guards which appeared empty, he was pleased to see healthy trees like the oak in our photograph. In a summer of intense heat and severe drought this success is remarkable, although in general our Welsh sites seemed to have fared better than the rest of the UK.

There was dense vegetation in places, particularly brambles and grass, but our saplings are growing despite this. We saw some deer damage to tree guards but the guards had clearly done their job as we found no damage to the trees themselves.

The landowner had planted a small orchard of fruit trees on the edge of the woodland, and so far all of these trees have survived despite some heat stress.
