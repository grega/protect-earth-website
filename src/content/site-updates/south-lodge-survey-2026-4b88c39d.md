Each part of the site appears to be doing equally well here, with a survival rate of 82% thanks to our recent restock. The original survivors have grown wonderfully, and the majority of the restock are taking nicely too. Many of the older trees are growing out of the top of the guards, and when we looked inside the guards the others looked very healthy. Fortunately, the trees have not faced much competition from grass and weeds. Some guards had fallen over, so our surveyor did some quick maintenance to fix them.

We were pleased to see lots of self-seeded oaks appearing around the site, as these are likely to be hardy and should withstand weather conditions that planted saplings can find challenging.
