It was very exciting to see how much the trees have grown since last year. Although a small minority of the trees are showing some degree of stress heat, they seem to be coping. The rest appear to be completely healthy and unbothered by the drought, giving us a survival rate of 83%. The fence and gate continue to keep the sheep out of the planted area.

The successful species mix here was very apparent with each planned species well represented throughout. The cherry trees are thriving on this site, showing the biggest growth, with some big oaks too. The Scot’s pine were the only species underperforming at this point. However, all the trees were well above the guards.

Although this site doesn’t need a restock at the moment, it would be good if some maintenance can be carried out before next spring, perhaps by students from the college.
