A survival rate of only 44.8% at this site is incredibly disappointing, especially after our restocking and maintenance day earlier this year. As can be seen in the photographs, this site is exposed to the sun and incredibly dry after this record-breaking hot, dry summer. The saplings that have taken, are now well out of the guards. Some of the guards and trees show damage from rodents.

The gardeners working here are very enthusiastic and supportive, and really want to make this project work. They are very happy to help the trees and have the resources and time to do so. Acting on their own initiative they have started to mow around the trees and intend to mow around them all. We encouraged them to continue to do this, and to add mulch around all the healthy trees to suppress the grass further. We also advised them to keep the weeds in the middle of the plot down as this is where water pools in the winter. They suggested using root dip when planting the restock. This has worked very well for us on other sites with similar problems.

We will arrange a further restocking and maintenance session this winter and will offer further support as necessary.
