In July the landowner reported that the trees were doing alright, despite the prolonged dry spell. She was concerned that the leaves were already turning brown so we went to look for ourselves.

We were pleasantly surprised to find a survival rate of 95% in the first field and 96% in the second, although this was mainly thanks to our restock in March and the maintenance we had carried out at the same time. The new trees seem to have established well despite the drought this summer, and most of the older trees were well out of their guards and growing strongly. The site had received some rain, unlike our sites in the south, and this has clearly made a difference.
