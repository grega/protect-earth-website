This was our third attempt to carry out our spring maintenance at this property. Our two earlier attempts had been thwarted by the weather and unfortunately the corporate group who had planned to come were no longer available as we were now well into summer holiday season. Protect Earth took a team of four, and with the help of three local volunteers we got to work removing weeds and grass from inside the guards, straightening or replacing guards and stakes, and spreading mulch around the saplings. The landowner made the job infinitely easier by transporting all the mulch up to the planting site, then leaving it in convenient heaps for us. They also delivered a whole bunch of refreshments and lunch. Amazing support like this really keeps our volunteers going, especially when it’s so hot!

We had 3 out of 3 volunteers show up for the whole day, and one volunteer was a top-notch spotter of natural regeneration saplings! We guarded and mulched these additional saplings to protect them too. The landowners provided an excellent lunch and drinks throughout the day.

With the small number of volunteers we were only to get about about half of the job done, despite everyone working really hard. We have planned another session in September once the school holidays are over, and hopefully the weather is cooler.
