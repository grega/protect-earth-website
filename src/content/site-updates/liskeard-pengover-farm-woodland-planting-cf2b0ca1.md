After planting the hedgerow the day before, we returned on this wet Sunday morning to plant the small woodland on our neighbours’ farm.  Unfortunately, only one volunteer turned up to help, despite others having signed up. This does happen from time to time, especially on wet Sundays, but we carried on regardless.

We dotted the saplings at random, roughly three metres apart, with shrubs in clusters and around the perimeter of the new planting to provide protection, using a ratio of 70% trees to 30% shrubs. We left some gaps, especially on the eastern edge of the new planting near existing trees. With a low stocking density of 1111 trees per hectare there will be plenty of opportunity for natural regeneration from the adjoining woodland area.

Our sole volunteer was friendly and worked well with the rest of the team, even saying that they would be happy to volunteer with us again.
