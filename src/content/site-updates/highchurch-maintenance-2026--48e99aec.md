We had a good number of volunteers signed up for this event, but as is often the case, not all of them turned up on the day. We had four volunteers for the whole day and a couple more turned up to help in the afternoon. Despite that, we were able to carry out work on around a quarter of the site, focusing mostly on the new trees planted in the restock in February 2026, clearing competing grass, docks, etc. from around them and putting on spiral guards where needed.

Once we were happy the volunteers knew what they were doing (they were great) our team leader and the landowner did a stint each on the brush cutters, clearing back the heavy areas of docks that were swamping some of the smaller trees. There's a lot of new wildflower diversity in the field - thistles, buttercups, vetch and so on coming up between the grass.

The volunteers all said they enjoyed it.  The landowner provided tea, coffee, biscuits and water to keep the volunteers going, He had also set out hay bales in the barn, but the team were happy to work on and we stayed down in the field, taking shade under the Land Rover for breaks and drinks. We worked on until about half past three, by which time most people had had enough as it was quite a warm day.
