This site was the reason that Protect Earth came into existence in the first place. Whilst staying on this farm our chairman-to-be learned from the farmer how hard it could be to plant trees because of government red tape, so he decided he could make life simpler for small landowners. Together with like-minded friends across the UK, Protect Earth was born. Once the charity was officially registered we went to plant trees here on our first site.

As this was new to us, we started small, planting a mix of just 114 native saplings on this farm to link small areas of woodland, which it was hoped would encourage regeneration and greater biodiversity over time. We chose species carefully to provide a range of food and shelter for wildlife.
