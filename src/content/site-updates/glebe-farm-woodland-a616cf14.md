The landowner already had some trees and shrubs ready to plant, and we had more delivered, as well as stakes and guards, to make the numbers up to 600. The plan was to create three small areas of woodland to create a new wildlife habitat to improve biodiversity on the farm. When the planting sites are spread out planting takes longer as it involves more walking and transporting of equipment so we planned a two-day event. We planted with help from four corporate volunteers from Hoffmann UK Quality Tools Ltd. on the first day and ten from ChilliBob PR and Marketing and their clients on the following day.

Our volunteers had this to say. “Location was described beforehand so we knew where to go. Good training on how to plant trees and the day was fun!” “Really enjoyed this and would be willing to do this again in the future.”
